# Week 1 Plan

Three episodes, one every two days. YouTube Shorts first, then Instagram and Facebook Reels. Nothing posts without approval.

## Episode 1 - Taxi Chura Li, Phir Pata Chala Papa Hain!

Status: script, prompts and posting notes done in `hindi-taxi-reel/`. Full direction in `hindi-taxi-reel/direction.md`.

- Premise: woman hails a taxi in the rain, a man steals it, twist - he is her dad.
- Hook: "Ye meri taxi thi!" over the slamming taxi door.
- Climax: "Papa? AAP?" / "Beta, taxi to maine hi book ki thi."
- Sticky end: "Papa bhi na..." + caption "Tag your papa."
- Post first. This is the sample episode.

## Episode 2 - Papa Roz Do Tiffin Kyun Le Jaate Hain?

- Premise: daughter notices papa packing a second tiffin every morning. She is sure he has a secret second family, or a girlfriend. She follows him.
- Hook (0-2s): her whispering to camera, "Papa roz do tiffin le jaate hain. Aaj peecha karungi."
- Layering: morning kitchen sounds, her sneaking behind him, tense music, on-screen text counting the clues.
- Climax: he is not meeting anyone. He hands the second tiffin to the old watchman at the building gate, who eats alone every day.
- Emotion: suspicion turns into pride. Her line: "Papa, aap ye roz karte ho?" His line: "Koi poochta nahi usse, beta."
- Sticky end: watchman smiling, eating. Caption: "Tag the papa who gives quietly."
- Title: "Papa Ka Secret Tiffin!"

## Episode 3 - Bhai Ne Bola Fail Ho Gaya

- Premise: younger brother comes home, head down, says he failed his exam. The whole house panics. Mummy cries, papa goes silent.
- Hook (0-2s): his face, flat voice: "Mummy, main fail ho gaya."
- Layering: the house going quiet, mummy's worry, papa's silence, sad music, on-screen text: "Ghar me sannata."
- Climax: he holds up the marksheet. He did not fail. He topped the class. He was joking. Twist line: "Arre, top kiya hai! Mazaak kar raha tha!"
- Emotion: panic turns into relief and laughter, then pride. Papa's line: "Aaj se tera naam topper."
- Sticky end: the family laughing, marksheet on screen. Caption: "Tag your bhai who does this."
- Title: "Bhai Fail Nahi, Topper Nikla!"

## Cadence

- Day 1: Episode 1 posts.
- Day 3: Episode 2 posts.
- Day 5: Episode 3 posts.
- Day 6: review the three in YouTube Studio, keep the one lesson each taught, plan Week 2.

Full scripts for Episodes 2 and 3 get written once Episode 1 is approved for production, in the same shape as the Episode 1 sample.
