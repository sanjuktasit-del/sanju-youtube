# Asha Kaun Hai? — Episode 1

**Format:** vertical 9:16, ~60 seconds, Hinglish, AI drama series.
**Channel:** The Swinging Star (Sanju's personal YouTube).
**Status:** script + direction + prompts ready. No video file exists yet — scenes are generated in Dreamina (free tier) from the prompts in `dreamina-prompts.md`, or via the Kaggle pipeline once it is set up.

## The story in one line

Meera comes to hear her father's will. Her name is not in it. Everything goes to a woman nobody has heard of: Asha.

## Beat 1 (0-8s) — Rain. Night. Mansion gate.

Visual: Meera (late 20s) stands at a tall iron gate in heavy rain, drenched, holding a folder to her chest.

Meera (voiceover, calm, cold): "Papa ki will padhne aayi thi. Pata nahi tha... will mein mera naam hi nahi hai."

On-screen text: "PAPA KI WILL. MERA NAAM NAHI."

## Beat 2 (8-20s) — The will reading.

Visual: long dining table, warm lamp light. Lawyer (60s, glasses) reads from papers. Meera sits opposite her stepmother Rekha (early 50s, silk saree, still as stone).

Lawyer: "Saari property, saara paisa... Asha ke naam."

Silence.

Meera: "Asha? Yeh Asha kaun hai?"

Rekha (small smile): "Yeh toh tumhare papa hi jaante the."

## Beat 3 (20-32s) — The photo.

Visual: Meera alone in her father's study, rain on the window. She opens an old drawer and finds a photograph: her father, young, holding a baby girl. She flips it over. Handwriting on the back: "Asha, meri beti."

Meera (voiceover, shaken): "Meri ek behen thi... aur mujhe pata bhi nahi tha."

On-screen text: "ASHA, MERI BETI."

## Beat 4 (32-45s) — The confrontation.

Visual: Meera holds the photo up to Rekha in the hallway.

Meera: "Aap jaanti thi, hai na?"

Rekha: "Tumhare papa ne mujhse bhi chhupaya tha."

Meera: "Toh phir... Asha kahan hai?"

Rekha looks toward the main door. A beat.

Rekha: "Woh aaj raat aa rahi hai."

## Beat 5 (45-60s) — The doorbell.

Visual: doorbell rings. Meera opens the main door. Rain behind. A woman stands there, drenched, simple white kurta, same eyes as the father in the photo.

Woman: "Main Asha hoon."

Cut to black.

On-screen text: "PART 2 — KAUN HAI ASHA?"

End card: "Part 2 ke liye comment karo: PART 2"

## Characters

- **Meera:** late 20s, Indian, long black hair, maroon salwar suit (dry indoors), drenched in rain scenes.
- **Rekha (stepmother):** early 50s, sharp, silk saree, cold and still.
- **Lawyer:** 60s, glasses, grey suit.
- **Asha:** late 20s, same eyes as the father, simple white kurta, drenched.
- **Father:** only in the old photograph, young, holding a baby girl.
