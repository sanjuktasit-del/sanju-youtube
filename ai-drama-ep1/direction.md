# Asha Kaun Hai? — Episode 1, direction

## Look

- Vertical 9:16, cinematic. Night and rain throughout.
- Cold blue rain light outside, warm lamp light inside. The contrast is the mood.
- Hard cuts between beats. No dissolves. On-screen text large, white, centred, 2 lines max.

## Character lock (repeat in every generation prompt)

- Meera: Indian woman, late 20s, long black hair, maroon salwar suit.
- Rekha: Indian woman, early 50s, silk saree, sharp, cold expression.
- Lawyer: Indian man, 60s, glasses, grey suit.
- Asha: Indian woman, late 20s, simple white kurta, same eyes as the father.
- Father: only in the old photograph.

Generate a character reference image first (Meera, front-facing, dry, neutral light) and use image-to-video for each scene. Keep faces in 3/4 or profile in the rain scenes and avoid fast head turns — that is where faces break.

## Sound

- One low tense drone under beats 1 to 4.
- A single music sting exactly at "Main Asha hoon."
- Silence on the cut to black.
- Dialogue and voiceover are added in CapCut after the scenes are generated, not baked into the video generation.

## Rules

- No child characters, no violence on screen, no kidnapping. The drama is the secret, not a crime.
- Dialogue stays Hinglish as written in the script.
- Nothing posts without Sanju's approval. She sees the cut before it goes up.
