# Sanju YouTube - End-to-End Playbook

How every video on this channel gets made, from idea to posting. Personal channel, Hindi family comedy with a twist. Not UpBox.

## 1. Story

Every episode follows the same engine:

- **Relatable moment:** something every Indian family has lived through. Taxi, tiffin, exam, phone, chai.
- **Hook (first 2 seconds):** open mid-conflict, never at the beginning. A fight, a shock, a question. If the first 2 seconds do not create tension, reshoot the opening.
- **Layering:** four layers build together.
  - Visual: what the camera shows.
  - Dialogue: short lines, Hindi, the way people actually speak.
  - Sound: room sound, rain, horn, music sting. Silence is a layer too.
  - Text: bold on-screen captions for viewers watching muted.
- **Climax (around 80%):** the twist. One reveal that reframes everything before it. The twist must be hidden until this point.
- **Emotion:** the arc is always irritation or worry turning into warmth, laughter or pride. The laugh or the warm moment is what gets shared.
- **Sticky end:** the last frame loops back to the first, and the caption asks the viewer to do one thing: tag someone, comment one line, share with family. One ask only.

## 2. Direction

- Same actors, same locations, same props across all scenes of one episode.
- The twist is shot as one uncut close-up. One full second of silence before the reveal line. The pause is the joke landing.
- No hint of the twist before the climax. The twist character must read as a stranger until the reveal.
- Vertical 9:16, under 60 seconds, for YouTube Shorts.

## 3. Production

- **Dreamina:** generate Scene 1 first, use its first frame as the reference for the other scenes so faces and props do not change. One prompt per scene, kept in the episode folder.
- **CapCut:** cut to the dialogue, add bold white Hindi captions with black outline, keep the room sound under everything, one music sting exactly at the twist.
- **Check before posting:** faces stay the same across scenes, twist does not leak early, audio is your own voiceover or a licensed track, no reused clip from another creator.

## 4. Posting

Order for every episode:

1. YouTube Shorts first (growth + long-form funnel).
2. Instagram Reels (brand-deal audience, pays $0 per view).
3. Facebook Reels (older family audience, small payout).

TikTok is banned in India, so it is off the list for Hindi content aimed at India. Nothing posts without Sanju's approval.

## 5. Review

After each episode, check in YouTube Studio: views, average view duration, and where people drop off. If the drop is in the first 3 seconds, the hook failed. If the drop is before the twist, the middle sagged. Fix that one thing in the next episode. One lesson per video.
