# Episode 1 - Direction

## The shot list

- **Shot 1 (0-2s, hook):** close-up, her angry face, taxi door slamming. Her voice: "Ye meri taxi thi!" No establishing shot first. Start inside the fight.
- **Shot 2 (2-8s):** wide, rainy street, yellow taxi, her waving. Rain sound, taxi horn.
- **Shot 3 (8-20s):** medium, he steps in front of her, gets in, smug. His line: "Zara tez hona chahiye tha, sweetheart."
- **Shot 4 (20-35s):** tracking, she runs after the taxi, window rolls down. His line: "Arey, sambhal ke. Thand lag jayegi." Her line: "Aadmi aise hi kyun hote hain?"
- **Shot 5 (35-48s, climax):** one uncut close-up on her face as the door opens. One full second of silence. Then: "Papa? AAP?" His smile: "Beta, taxi to maine hi book ki thi."
- **Shot 6 (48-60s):** two-shot, both laughing in the back seat, rain on the window. Her line: "Bas, ho gaya mera." On-screen text: "Papa bhi na..."

## Emotion curve

Anger (shots 1-4) -> shock (shot 5, the silence) -> warmth (shot 6, the laugh). The laugh is the share trigger.

## Sound

- Rain under everything. Taxi horn once, at the steal.
- No music until the twist. One soft sting exactly when she says "Papa?" Then warm, light music under the laugh.
- Silence is doing work in shot 5. Do not fill it.

## CapCut notes

- Bold white Hindi captions, black outline, centre-bottom.
- Cut on the dialogue, not on the action. Let her angry face hold an extra half second in shot 1.
- Last frame loops to the first: rain, taxi, her waving.

## Thumbnail

Frame from shot 3, his smug face taking the taxi, text: "PAPA?!"
