# Dreamina Prompts - Hindi Taxi Reel

One prompt per scene. Generate each clip in vertical 9:16. Keep the same two actors and the same yellow taxi across all scenes.

## Scene 1 - Rainy street
"Cinematic vertical video, rainy night street in a city, a young Indian woman in a beige coat waving to hail a yellow taxi, wet road reflections, street lights, realistic, moody lighting"

## Scene 2 - The steal
"Cinematic vertical video, a middle-aged Indian man in a black puffer jacket stepping in front of a young woman and getting into a yellow taxi, she looks shocked, rainy night, realistic"

## Scene 3 - The chase
"Cinematic vertical video, young Indian woman running after a yellow taxi in the rain, the man rolls down the taxi window and smiles at her, night city street, realistic"

## Scene 4 - The twist
"Cinematic vertical video, inside a yellow taxi at night, a young Indian woman opens the door and looks shocked recognising the middle-aged man as her father, warm smile, rain on windows, realistic"

## Scene 5 - Punchline
"Cinematic vertical video, father and daughter laughing together in the back seat of a yellow taxi at night, rain on the window, warm light, realistic, heartwarming"

## Generation tips
- Generate Scene 1 first and use its first frame as the reference for the actors and taxi in the other scenes.
- If the faces change between scenes, regenerate that scene with the reference image attached.
- Keep each clip 6-10 seconds, then cut to the dialogue in CapCut.
