# YouTube Publishing Notes - Hindi Taxi Reel

## Format
- YouTube Short, vertical 9:16, under 60 seconds.
- Upload from the YouTube app or YouTube Studio as a new video. Vertical + under 60s is treated as a Short automatically.

1. Taxi Chura Li, Phir Pata Chala Papa Hain!
2. Taxi Meri Thi, Le Gaya Papa!
3. Baarish, Taxi Aur Papa Ka Twist

Baarish wali raat, ek taxi, aur ek aisa twist jo aapne socha nahi hoga! Papa bhi na...

## Thumbnail
Use a frame from Scene 2 (his smug face taking the taxi) with the text: "PAPA?!"

## Before posting
- Watch the full video once: faces must stay the same across scenes, and the twist must not leak early.
- Check the audio is your own voiceover or a licensed track. Do not reuse the original reel audio.
- Do not re-upload the Florss Arts reel. This remake is yours; the original is theirs.
