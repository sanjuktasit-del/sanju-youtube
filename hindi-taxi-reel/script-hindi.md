# Hindi Taxi Reel - Script

A Hindi remake of the taxi mini-film idea: a woman hails a taxi in the rain, a man steals it, then the twist - he is her dad.

Format: vertical 9:16, under 60 seconds, for YouTube Shorts.

## Scene 1 - Rainy street (0-8s)
Visual: night, rain, a young woman in a beige coat waving at a yellow taxi.
Dialogue (her): "Taxi! Taxi!"

## Scene 2 - The steal (8-20s)
Visual: a man in a black puffer jacket steps in front of her and takes the taxi.
Dialogue (him, smug): "Zara tez hona chahiye tha, sweetheart."
Dialogue (her, angry): "Ye meri taxi thi!"

## Scene 3 - The chase (20-35s)
Visual: she runs after the taxi, he rolls the window down.
Dialogue (him): "Arey, sambhal ke. Thand lag jayegi, sweetheart."
Dialogue (her): "Aadmi aise hi kyun hote hain?"

## Scene 4 - The twist (35-48s)
Visual: the taxi stops, the door opens, she sees his face properly.
Dialogue (her, shocked): "Papa? AAP?"
Dialogue (him, smiling): "Beta, taxi to maine hi book ki thi."

## Scene 5 - Punchline (48-58s)
Visual: both laughing in the taxi, rain on the window.
Dialogue (her): "Bas, ho gaya mera. Main ja rahi hoon."
On-screen text: "Papa bhi na..."

## Notes
- Keep the twist hidden until Scene 4. No hint earlier that he is her father.
- The joke only works if the man looks like a stranger in Scenes 2-3.
- Original inspiration: Florss Arts taxi reel (Facebook). This is an original Hindi remake, not a re-upload.
